# Writing Workflows

## New Chapter

1. Read the context-loading recipe for continuing writing.
2. Confirm the target chapter number and title from `workbench/NEXT.md` or the user's request.
3. Create the chapter under `manuscript/<volume>/第NNN章-标题.md`.
4. Preserve front matter when present.
5. Write prose in-scene, not as outline summary.
6. Update `STATE.md`, `CHAPTERS.md`, and `workbench/CHANGELOG.md`.

## Prose Requirements

- Use character-perceived narration by default.
- Keep language concrete: action, sound, smell, temperature, weight, spatial relation.
- Avoid meta words in prose: 本章, 上一章, 下一章, 作者, 读者, 大纲, 设定, 剧情, 主角.
- Every chapter should advance at least one relationship, secret, danger, goal, or cost.
- Magic and the black sword must carry cost.

## Revision

When revising, preserve what already works. Change the smallest requested span. If continuity changes, update `CONTINUITY.md` or note the needed update in `workbench/CHANGELOG.md`.

## Planning

When planning, separate:

- External pressure
- Character relationship movement
- Mystery or information movement
- Cost or consequence
- Ending hook

Plans live in `outline/` or `workbench/`; prose lives only in `manuscript/`.
